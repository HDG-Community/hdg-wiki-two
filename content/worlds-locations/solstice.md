Solstice (LAKE-211c) is a habitable garden world located roughly 450 light years from Sol in the Cygnus Constellation. Originally settled by a breakaway group of Martian Quakers in 2454, it was later bought out in a hostile takeover by the Osbourne-Clarke conglomerate and used as a prison colony in hopes of eventually transforming the planet into a viable colony with a permanent underclass of prisoners serving as a workforce. Following a violent uprising in 2551, the planet was abandoned by Osbourne-Clarke and bombarded from space by the Cosmic Navy as a test for an experimental hypermetric weapon, the _Jormungandr_ RKV.

## History
Solstice formed between 3.5 and four billion years ago, and until recently hosted no sophont life whatsoever. Most forms of terrestrial macroscale life on the planet follow a tetrapodal model, similar to earth; however, virtually all sea life and some forms of terrestrial life use radial symmetry, a consequence of an extinction event causing a strong divergence between the two branches of life early in Solstice's prehistory. 
### Early History of Terran Settlement 
Solstice was first settled by a subsect of the Martian branch of the Religious Society of Friends (more often known as Quakers), the Friends Among the Stars. Their goal in doing so was to establish an agrarian society free from the exploitative and increasingly intrusive hypercapitalist boom-bust cycle that characterized pre-Accord human space, but without forsaking advances in medicine and technology to do so. Using seed funds from the original church fellowship in Elysium Valles on Mars, the colonial venture invested wisely over the course of nearly a century as they waited for surveys to find an ideal world for their project. Eventually, in 2451, LAKE-211 was found to have a habitable world orbiting it with minimal actuarial risk for asteroid or cometary encounters. The FAS purchased the colonial license for the system, giving it the name Fisher, a reference both to historical Quaker missionary Mary Fisher as well as to Christ's declaration in the Book of Matthew that He would make of His Apostles "fishers of men." The FAS also purchased a heavy long-haul freighter to serve as the colony transshipment vessel, which they named the _Swallow_ after the vessel that carried Mary Fisher to Boston. 

_Swallow_'s first voyage to Fisher concluded on May 12, 2454, and landfall was made on the side of what would become the town of Beacon three days later. This initial voyage consisted almost entirely of prefab structures, industrial equipment, and seed stores for the advance colonial party of 53 to begin developing the needed infrastructure to support a larger population. 

Over successive journeys, each taking nearly two months in each direction, Fisher slowly accrued a larger population, a greater technological and industrial base, and slowly began to branch outward from the original colony site into branch towns organized around developing and exploiting local resources. Unfortunately for the colony, they also began accruing debt. Operational and maintenance costs for the _Swallow_ rose faster than anticipated, and exports were both few and far between and did not sell nearly as well as the colony had hoped. This problem was only exacerbated following the establishment of the Terran Accord in $year, and the subsequent addition of franchise taxation to the colony's financial burden. 
#### The Osbourne-Clarke Takeover 
Osbourne-Clarke, a multi-system conglomerate with deep connections in the Early Accord, saw a potential opportunity in a distant colony in Cygnus, and with the increasing debt that Fisher was laboring under it proved simple to begin leveraging the colony. The company began by acquiring all that debt, either by purchasing it directly or acquiring the firms that held it. In 2477, they used that dept to leverage a buyout of the _Swallow_, promising to maintain transshipment of needed medical supplies and other essentials that the colony on Fisher could not yet produce for themselves. They then manufactured numerous delays, added surcharges, and generally made the contract with Osbourne-Clarke to operate the freighter as expensive for the colony as they could. Within three years, the colony approaching financial ruin and an epidemic of the flu wreaking havoc on the population, the colony leadership capitulated and sold the colonial franchise license to Osbourne-Clarke to clear the debt. 
### Osbourne-Clarke Era 
Osbourne-Clarke took official control of Fisher on July 30th, 2480, and ushered in a sweeping wave of changes to the colonial charter. Among other things, they renamed the planet to Solstice, moved the capital to the newly-established corporate offices in Landfall, on the far side of the $namehere Mountains, and began a general program of rewriting the history of the planet for marketing purposes, minimizing the contributions of the Quakers. They then began to lay in infrastructure for their operating vision for the colony: an open-air prison colony for "high risk" prisoners, typically political prisoners, who would serve as a labor force for a future high-net-worth colony. 

In order to retain as much of their labor force as possible, Osbourne-Clarke negotiated a contract wherein transport _to_ Solstice would be paid on contract with the sentencing authority, but transport _from_ Solstice was not. They then added a requirement to the standard sentencing contract absolving them of the responsibility to fund the return voyage at the end of a prisoner's sentence. Solstice quickly became notorious in the Terran core systems as a kind of Space Australia, transportation to which as a sentence for a crime was effectively a life sentence of exile and hard labor regardless of how many years the unfortunate victim was actually sentenced for. 

Under Osbourne-Clarke, the planet rapidly industrialized as the foundation for a new colony was built and the former inhabitants were marginalized. Services to the old Quaker sectors east of the mountains were minimal, and many of the youth drifted away to seek work from Osbourne-Clarke directly. Many became prison guards, while a few managed to win more lucrative contracts in higher positions. These positions usually had significant turnover, as once these workers could afford to leave a planet that was no longer theirs, they almost universally did so. By 2500, the Quaker population was less than half of what it had been; by 2520, the last of the holdouts either accepted relocation or had died of old age. 
#### Layla Al-Tabari 
Following her destruction of the Marama Logistics Platform in 2530 as an act of revenge for the murder of her mother by Accord peacekeeping forces, Layla Al-Tabari was sentenced to transportation to Solstice. Upon her arrival, recognizing her high intelligence and literacy, she was quickly snapped up to fill a post in the underserved Archives department in Landfall, a task at which she excelled and in which she had little oversight, due to her superior being an alcoholic and generally absent from his post. 

Over the next twenty years, using the revolutionary pseudonum Cassandra Hope, Layla Al-Tabari built a network of leftist circles across Solstice, either taking advantage of the high density of leftist political prisoners or educating them herself from memory. By the time of the Terran Pacification Campaign, this network stretched across Solstice and had broad support from the incarcerated population. While this network remained largely quiescent, this period of time marked a slow increase in sabotage, theft, and generally subtle action against Osbourne-Clarke, especially in the more distant eastern region formerly settled by the Quakers. 
### Revolutionary Era 
Following first contact with the Affini, the Terran Cosmic Navy pulled the small fleet stationed around Solstice and sent it to the front. Without the military to back them up, the time was ripe for open action against Osbourne-Clarke. Over the course of five months, an insurgency rapidly displaced the overwhelmed guard force and seized control of the planet, beginning in outlying regions and advancing towards Landfall. The last shuttle carrying Osbourne-Clarke loyalists left the surface on June 11th, 2551, and following sabotage to a few key orbital communications satellites, the corporate leadership of the planet departed the system. 
#### The Loyalists' Voyage
With nothing more than a jump capable yacht that could not support the number of Osbourne-Clarke loyalists that had fled the planet, the journey to a friendly port was a harrowing one. The loyalists endured intolerable conditions as the overloaded environmental system struggled to scrub CO2 from the air and the radiators struggled to vent waste heat (a problem compounded with every jump made). It took nearly a month for the little ship, designed mostly for sightseeing jaunts in the frontier, to make it to Cosmic Naval Station Tyndareus, a hundred light years distant. 

From Tyndareus, using rapid drone relay, the news of the uprising's success (and, indeed, of the uprising itself; management had concealed the size of the problem for fear of consequences from above) was sent to Osbourne-Clarke headquarters in Epsilon Eridani. Once informed, the Board of Directions quickly reached a decision that would have enduring consequences for Solstice. 
#### Revolutionary Government 
An interim government was established in Landfall by the revolutionaries shortly after their successful ouster of the Osbourne-Clarke government, consisting of representatives from all the major settlements. This interim council, however, was hampered by political infighting between different leftist tendencies, all of which had differing visions for how a permanent revolutionary government should look. Negotiations and discussions were still ongoing on August 29th, when the CNS _Intransigent_ arrived in orbit. 
#### Jormungandr and the Long Winter  
The Jormungandr Self-Contained Relativistic Kill Vehicle was an experimental superweapon platform developed for the Terran Cosmic Navy by Irvine Aerospace. The device used an intentionally mis-constructed jump drive designed to fail in a specific way as a large-scale shaped charge, which imparted a massive metric-kinetic charge to a 50-kilogram tungsten slug that would near-instantly accelerate it to nearly the speed of light. The weapon was fantastically expensive, prone to faults, and horrendously innacurate - however, it was also less expensive than maintaining a dedicated rail-artillery ship, could be carried even by smaller ships, and most importantly of all, made a very satisfying visual display that the Admiralty really liked. 

Solstice remains one of two systems, along with 26 Cygni, where the Jormungandr is known to have been deployed in combat in. On August 29th, after using drones to wipe out what little remained of Solstice's orbital communications infrastructure, the _Intransigent_ deployed and fired a Jormungandr in what was officially marked down as a "weapons test."

The device performed as expected, firing a tungsten slug that entered Solstice's atmosphere above Landfall at 3:21 PM, local time, immediately causing a wave of fusion reactions as it did so. The resulting explosion was large enough to not only vaporize Landfall entirely, but to set off firestorms across the continent and launch a tremendous amount of ejecta into the upper atmosphere, where it lingered for years blocking out sunlight. 

The remnants of the revolutionary government, including Layla Al-Tabari, organized mutual support networks and an organized defense, fearing that the Accord would land ground troops shortly after. No such invasion manifested, however, and the revolutionaries were left to fend for themselves on a rapidly cooling planet. 

Estimates indicate that anywhere from 10 to 15,000 people were immediately killed in the strike, and upwards of 10,000 additional deaths were caused in the firestorms and the subsequent food shortage. 
### Affini Era 
_This section, and subsequent sections, contains spoilers for the plot of No Gods, No Masters._

As the Affini swept inward from the core, they detected no emissions from Solstice, and therefore deprioritized it in favor of populated worlds. It took until the integration of Epsilon Eridani and the Sol System, when the central legal database of the Accord and of Osbourne-Clarke's operations were collated by the Office of Transitional Neoxenoveterinary Archeobureaucracy, that Solstice's existence even came to light. 

The floret tender _Tillandsia_ was tasked for the mission to investigate and, if necessary, provide support, now that primary suppression operations were winding to a close and the need for overflow capacity for large-population feralist captures had diminished. _Tillandsia_ arrived at Solstice in late June of 2554, just as the planet was beginning to thaw for what little summer it could expect. While initially the revolutionary government entered negotiations with the Affini, the decision was shortly thereafter made to hide from the Affini in an attempt to retain autonomy by making the effort to root out and capture the population more expensive in terms of time and resources than the Affini were willing to invest. 

Needless to say, this failed. 

Intensive climate remediation efforts began shortly afterward, and by 2557 Solstice experienced its first real summer in more than a half a decade. The planet's biodiversity had plummeted in the mass extinction caused by the impact winter, but Affini ecological engineers were able to restore most species to life and fill all necessary niches in the biosphere. Following the conclusion of the ecological remediation and the arrival of full-time relief infrastructure, the _Tillandsia_ departed its position to rejoin Survey Fleet Telonema. 
## Geography 
Solstice is a relatively standard Habitable-Zone terrestrial world, with a significant hydrosphere covering nearly 80% of the planet's surface, active plate tectonics, and a magnetic field produced by a spinning molten core. Its Van Allen Belts are slightly more radioactive than Earth's due to the system primary star's increased stellar flux, but not to a degree dangerous to transit. 

Solstice has one large continent in the northern hemisphere that contains roughly 75% of all landmass on the planet. This landmass boasts several spectacular mountain ranges that divide it up into a broader western plain and a smaller (but still quite expansive) valley in the east. These mountain ranges help to buffer the region against the convective storms produced in the immense ocean. 

There are several much smaller subcontinental land-masses and a host of islands in the southern hemisphere, but none of these have ever been inhabited. All are presently designated as Native Biome Preservation Territories, and due to their decreased exposure to the Long Winter, were instrumental in reviving much of Solstice's biosphere. 
### Western Plains Communities 
#### New Landfall 
Established around the rim of the flooded crater bay where Landfall once stood, New Landfall is the only community on the planet constructed entirely according to Affini design sensibilities. It is highly decentralized, walkable, and boasts multiple transit networks enabling easy access from any part of the city to any other. 

New Landfall is the largest settlement on the planet, both by population and by area, and has been steadily growing in both respects since its inception in 2557. The city also serves as the landward anchor for the Parthenocissus space elevator and station, making it the primary transshipment point for cargo and population to and from the surface. 
#### Bulwark Revolutionary History Museum 
Little more than a old and carefully preserved bunker and a smattering of habs, service buildings, and the like, the former heart of the revolutionary-era survivorship is now a museum dedicated to the memory of those who perished in the revolution and the aftermath. 
#### Featherstone 
Westernmost of all settlements, Featherstone lies in the foothills of the Western Barrier Mountains. Originally established as a remote agricultural testing station, it was planned to develop into a fully self-sustaining exurb for emigrees following the opening of the colony by Osbourne-Clarke. Numerous buildings were constructed or partially constructed for this purpose, but the anticipated colonial windfall never came. 
### Elysium Valley Communities 
#### Beacon
The first terran settlement on the planet, Beacon is an intentional community designed as much as possible to resemble an earth-to-mid-20th Century New England coastal town.

## Demographics
As a Terran colony predating domestication, the primary xenosophont population of Solstice is unsurprisingly Terrans, who compose an overwhelming majority. A few enclaves of other xenospecies exist, almost exclusively in New Landfall and on Parthenocissus Station and usually consisting of florets brought to the planet by their Affini owners. 

As Solstice was operated as a prison, its population was drawn from a diverse background across multiple star systems; however, the vast majority of Solsticers hail from average-to-high-gravity worlds, due to Solstice's near-Earth gravity being largely unsuitable for low-gravity adapted individuals. Standard English remains the most common language spoken on Solstice, but some communities use other languages in concert with or as an alterative to English.
## Ecology
Like most garden worlds, Solstice boasts extensive biodiversity, and while that was negatively affected by the Long Winter, careful management of the local biosphere and climate by the Affini Compact has restored virtually all of Solstice's former biodiversity. 
### Flora 
#### False Oak (Nonquercus fragrans)
Despite presenting a striking similarity to the Terran Oak, the False Oak is actually the visible portion of an underground mycelial superorganism that can stretch for kilometers. Its spores have a uniquely pungent (and often regarded as unpleasant) scent that is stronger in areas with large concentrations of False Oaks, but is generally appreciable throughout the ecosystem. 

False Oak is not edible, and while it makes for decent fuel, using it as such creates a smell that is compared unfavorably to the fungus' spores. During the Osbourne-Clarke era, the company attempted to exterminate the False Oak, but in doing so discovered that, like most fungal analogues across the galaxy, it cannot be killed in a way that matters. 
### Fauna
#### Tortoipus (Iatromolluscus birgusimillarus)
The tortoipus is an amphibious cephalopod-analogue with a thick chitinous shell. Able to breathe air and carry a supply of water within its internal anatomy to maintain appropriate hydration, the tortoipus is known for trekking up to a hundred and fifty kilometers inland in search of food and reproductive company, occupying a similar niche to the Terrestrial coconut crab. It is fond of warm temperatures and is known for sunning itself on convenient rocks or roads. 

Despite the cold of the Long Winter taking a toll on the tortoipus population, it recovered without significant assistance from Affini ecological management, and is rapidly becoming a nuisance and a staple cage fishing crop once more. 
#### Fruithawk (Fructoptera inconcinnus)
The fruithawk is an avian analogue known primarily as a nuisance to fruit cultivation. All flying species on Solstice evolved from a common ancestor, a small mammal-analogue with insectile wings, though the fruithawk has diverged from that ancestor significantly enough to be seen as a bird by the original Quaker colonists.

Fruithawks, much like certain terrestrial wasps, lay their eggs in overripe fruit. They do so by diving into them and puncturing them with a rigid ovipositor, leaving a characteristic divot. Unfortunately, the fruithawk is not particularly _good_ at locating fruit that are sufficiently overripened to serve as a suitable growth medium for their eggs, and so will simply ricochet from fruit to fruit until they find one that is. 

While fruithawks are not normally dangerous to humans, they will occasionally exhibit a magpie-like "swooping" behavior if, for example, they think a human's hat looks like a particularly breedable piece of fruit. Fruithawk "stings" are not venomous, and their eggs cannot survive the normal pH balance of human tissue, but the puncture wounds can still be quite nasty and hurt rather a lot. 
## Arts & Culture 
### Timekeeping 
The Quaker colonists who first arrived on Solstice established the original Solstice Calendar, which used a Martian standard calendrical method of synchronizing local time to Earth time - that is, each day ends with a timeslip where the clocks stop counting time for roughly one hour and twenty-four minutes. To account for the extra 14 days in the calendar, a two-week month was added each year between December and January, preventing calendrical drift. This method of timekeeping generates an eventual discrepancy in the age of any given person or object tracked via Solstice time, wherein an additional year must be counted for roughly every five years and 10 months on Solstice. Most clocks on Solstice, as throughout much of the Terran Accord, have a secondary display mode tracking Terran time, so the standard year is still generally well-known across the planet. 

During the Osbourne-Clarke Era, the company attempted to run the planet on Martian Standard Time, which suffered less of a discrepancy than Terran time would have but introduced other complications, such as the fact that Mars's year is twice as long as Solstice's or the constant drift of seasons across the calendar. The OC calendar was largely abandoned following the Revolutionary Era, and the original Quaker calendar remains in use today. 
### Sports
Most organized sports enjoyed on Solstice are imports from Accord worlds, including football, Martian rugby, and several stick-and-ball variants such as baseball and cricket. Cross-country and Telemark skiing are also extremely popular, being critical skills developed during the Long Winter that remain in active use in mountainous regions during colder months. 

Active sporting groups are most common in and around New Landfall and on Parthenocissus, with several small leagues operating year-round. New sports occasionally arrive from out-system and enjoy a surge of popularity before finding an equilibrium. 

The Solstice Games are a biennial amateur competition featuring numerous traditional sports. Alongside standard track and field displays, the Solstice Games are also known for a rotating slate of strategy games, marksmanship trials, motorsport, and esports. 
## Government
Solstice is governed by a joint committee of the People's Revolutionary Community Representative Causus and the Standing Committee on Integrated Sophont Needs for Solstice, the latter having a controlling veto on all business under the terms of the Solstice Domestication Treaty, (largely based on the Human Domestication Treaty). The PRCRC is comprised of representatives from individual communities on Solstice, who largely manage their own affairs in concert with local branches of the Affini Bureaucracy. 

This is a non-standard arrangement in Terran Space, made possible through the separate Solstice Domestication Treaty and the willingness of the local xenosophont population to engage in dialogue following initial difficulties largely fueled by a trauma-based reaction to outside authority. Subsequent followups by the [[bxwc|Bureau for Xenosophont Wellness]] and the Nascent Feralism Pollarding Office have been highly successful in ensuring that repeats of this event do not trouble the population. 
## Transportation
Solstice has an extensive road network that predates domestication, largely constructed by Osbourne-Clarke. However, older roads also exist in the Elysium Valley, dating to the Quaker colony. Both sets of road networks have been maintained and modernized for individual use, given the widespread nature of the population and the scenic journeys it allows for across largely undeveloped land. 

All major communities are connected via a maglev rail network. In addition, there exists a robust local community of flight enthusiasts, and personal air transports are not uncommon. In additional, coastal communities, especially in the region of New Landfall, usually have a strong boating culture, though this is seasonal due to the regular convection storms produced by the planet's equatorial regions. 
## Orbital Infrastructure 
### Parthenocissus Station & Elevator 
Connected to New Landfall by a space elevator, Parthenocissus Station is a medium-sized station constructed in the form a cylindrical habitat (this being a form of space habitat common to terran culture prior to domestication, and chosen for familiarity). Each of its seven habitation rings are nearly fifty kilometers across, with a diameter nearly as long. Much of the interior remains pristine and undeveloped, having been constructed with an eye toward long-term growth; it is expected that, in about 5,000 years, it may be necessary to consider adding on to the station, but by then it's likely other stations will have been established in the system, so plans for further development have been placed on hold for now.
