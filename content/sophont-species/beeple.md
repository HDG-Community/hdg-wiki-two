Beeple are an insectile social species, and the first xenosophonts the Affini ever domesticated. They are short, soft, cute, cuddly, and completely safe. They are also immortal, drug-fueled, hedonistic omniphiles with no attachment to concepts of independence or sobriety.

### Quick Info
**Homeworld**: Unknown, but the same homeworld as the Affini  
**Home System**: The Core Worlds (Triangulum Galaxy)  
**Native Gravity:** Roughly 0.3g

**Circadian Rhythm:** Variable  
**Lifespan:** Immortal  
**Domesticated:** Universally

**Names:** Beeple have no standard naming convention; they take whatever name their owners, caretakers, or communities deem is suitable for them. They most often take names that will be easily used in the communities they join. Many beeple names in Terran will be intentionally uncapitalized, to emphasize their status as pets.
Examples from HDG stories: bumble, macaroon, Montsechia

## Appearance & Physiology
As the first florets of the Compact, beeple have been around so long that they have no one single form factor. However, most beeple in Terran space, either coincidentally or on purpose, bear a striking resemblance to anthropomorphic Terran honeybees. They average around one meter in height, with fuzzy two-segmented bodies connected by a thick waist. They have six segmented limbs - two legs and four arms - with trilaterally symmetric hands ending in three short, stubby fingers.  Beeple heads are proportionally large for their size, and they have large compound eyes and short fuzzy antennae. Beeple are typically colored like Terran bees in yellow and black, but can be any color at all.

Beeple anatomy has been carefully engineered over tens of thousands of years to maximize their capacity to be cute, adorable, and as cuddly as possible. Beeple are adapted to be appealing to both affini and xenosophonts alike. The specifics of their original physiology are intentionally left vague, but in their current state, they are not only maximally designed for adorability, but also for  susceptibility to affini manipulation and compatibility with xenodrugs.

Beeple honey, being made mostly from the pollen of the xenodrug-producing flowers of the affini around them, is often a remarkably potent mixture to xenosophonts. When refined down, it is sometimes used to create class-O xenodrugs. Beeple, of course, vape it raw, because they haven't taken a tolerance break since before the advent of human agriculture.

The highly mutable nature of phyto-tech physiology makes beeple wildly variable not only in their appearance, but in durability as well. Beeple are capable of much more _intense_ forms of play than most xenosophonts, and some can come to expect (or demand) extreme treatment from affini to provide them with interesting experiences. Violently disassembling a beeple, flooding its brain with panic chemicals, and scrambling its ability to feel pleasure and pain might be considered by some beeple to be a good start to a lovely playdate.

The Beeple are often eldritch and ancient beings, having been entirely removed from all threat of danger, scarcity, or sobriety for over a hundred thousand years. They are entirely devoted and reverent to the Affini, and entirely committed to the Compact and their mission.

Many authors represent this show of reverence by only capitalizing words in beeple dialogue if it directly relates to an affini. Yet, beeple are also known to sass Affini more than most florets, because the two have such a close relationship. Beeple are deeply integrated within affini culture, and while many florets contribute to the Compact, Beeple are frequently found in roles of high trust. However, you'll find just as many partying or napping wrapped in vines.

Beeple are wholeheartedly devoted to the affini, the Compact, and its mission of universal domestication. It would not be a stretch to say that many beeple do not believe in the concept of independence, and feel that all life would be much better off as owned pets. For their part, many affini would not hesitate to point to these hedonistic, dependent drugsluts as a perfect role model for any newly-domesticated xenosophont species to emulate.

### Domestication

Beeple are unique among the Compact's xenosophonts in that they evolved to be florets, and are the base upon which affini define what florethood even is for other species. Many common hallmarks of florethood are remnants of the ancient affini's evolved behaviors with the beeple. For example, xenodrug intoxication was a method that caused beeple to be more clumsy, and therefore spread more of the affini's pollen.

Beeple are often depicted as favoring more communal arrangements, treating Affini as a whole as their owners, in contrast to the individualized affini/floret domestication dynamic that is common in Terrans. In many beeple communities, all affini provide a 'hive' of care and ownership for all florets, rather than single affini holding sole ownership of a pet.

As the Affini's oldest and most well-established pet species, beeple are the most numerous and widespread xenosophonts in the Compact. Many affini seek out beeple pets for their familiarity and ease of care. However, beeple can also be extraordinary challenges to an affini's caretaking drives. Some beeple will present themselves to their nurturers as a challenge to be tackled with all an affini's capacity, knowing that it is the affini's responsibility to provide them with all that a floret deserves... and that they, as florets, of course deserve everything.

## A beeple is forever

Many affini struggle with the idea of the loss of one of their beloved florets. The very idea of being separated from them is a difficult concept to bear; many stories focus almost entirely on the premise of a youngbloom recovering from the loss of their first pet for this exact reason. One might think there would be apt temptation to acquire a beeple as a pet. They are, after all, immortal.

But forever is a long time. A Beeple will never grow old, lose function, or pass away; an owned beeple is an eternal commitment to care and protection, as one immortal being to another. The relationship/dynamic may change over time, but the affini will always be responsible for the floret they have sewn their seed into.
