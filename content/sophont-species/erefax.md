Erefax are a quadrupedal species of large stature, sometimes known affectionately as “sheeposaurs" or "dollsheep". Before the Affini, their lives were long and slow, and now they are longer and slower.

### Quick Info
**Homeworld:** Ttellir, the fifth planet orbiting its star. A terrestrial silicate planet with one moon.   
**Home System:** Tchrrellit, a white Class-M star roughly 100ly from Sag A*  
**Native Gravity:** Roughly 0.7g

**Circadian Rhythm:** 57 Terran Hours  
**Lifespan:** Appx. 200-300 Terran years in the wild, upwards of 700 with current biotechnology. As of 2550, the oldest erefax remember their pre-compact childhoods  
**Domesticated:** 40%

**Names:** Due to their unique tongue structure, erefax languages have a high degree of phonetic complexity, creating a higher number of plosives, rhotics, and fricatives than 95% of other xenosophont species are able to produce. Their writing systems tend towards the symbological and featural, since their exponentially larger phonetic inventory make alphabets harder to use. Their names make good use of this expanded inventory.
Examples: Ttellir, Kht'teela, Rruhan

## Appearance and Physiology
Erefax are an ovine species of quadruped xenosophont, roughly the size and stature of a large moose from Terra. They stand roughly 3m tall at the shoulder, and weigh an average of 2000kg. Their notable features include their fleece, which covers their entire bodies and highly resembles the wool of Terran sheep or goats; their ceratopsid crests, which grow throughout their lives and are highly variable in shape and number of horns; and their prehensile, highly complicated tongues.

The most significant aspect of their physiology is the lanolin of their wool, which evolved as a deterrent against predators. It contains significant quantities of sedatives and muscle relaxants, which would put assaulting predators in a euphoric, paralytic state. The quantity and potency of lanolin produced depends on individual physiology and pheromonal state; females in heat produce much more potent lanolin in higher quantities, which “locks” their mates in place during mating.

Erefax crests are highly variable in shape, size, and number of horns, and are significantly affected by their physical and mental condition. Outside stresses contribute to shaping their crests as well. A large, complex crest signifies an elderly or pampered erefax, and a small, simple one indicates an erefax who is either very young or has endured a great deal of hardship and malnutrition. During hormonal rushes, mating displays, or other forms of stimulation, an erefax’s crest may become flushed with blood to the point of becoming a secondary erogenous zone. 

Erefax remain quadrupedal, but have highly complex prehensile tongues, which furcate at multiple points and have rough papillae to grip and manipulate tools. 


## Culture and History


Erefax place significant importance on living a slow, methodical existence. Trial and error is discouraged in favor of stability and meticulous planning. Their technological advancement occurred more slowly than Terra’s, but with more robust, reliable results. Interstellar probes had been sent out for nearly a century before the first individual erefax traveled into space, and then space travel was perfected for three more centuries before they colonized their homeworld’s only moon.

That emphasis on wisdom and slow existence displays itself most notably in the culture around erefax frills, which continue to grow and change throughout their lives. More elaborate and unique frills show a more varied and experienced life, painting those individuals as elders to be admired and respected. As erefax civilization grew, deliberate crest shaping became a sign of class and prestige. Butting heads and locking horns also became a common way of erefax challenging each other and resolving minor social disputes. 

Erefax evolved as a herd species, and as such large families and herds became the core of erefax social structures. An erefax home typically houses three or more generations of the same family, and a family will often stay within the same home for centuries at a time. As populations grew and class divisions formed, conflicts between herd families became more frequent and deadly. Erefax families are matrilineal, with the Herdmother being the head of the family. 

The erefax domesticated many of the native fauna of their world, including Frritis: Cat-like creatures the size of a greyhound that used to prey upon erefax flocks. Though they no longer need to guard their grain, they still keep these as pets, and since the word is technically unpronounceable by species with a single tonguetip, they are known as Flits in Terran-Rinan space.


### Domestication

A majority of erefax accepted and embraced the presence of the Affini when they arrived, but a significant minority harbored feralist sentiments. The erefax emphasis on slow, methodical existence meant that many rejected the sudden and all-encompassing change the Affini signified. 

The cognitive dissonance of a civilization that operated on much longer, slower timescales yet introduced such overwhelming change so quickly was a hard pill for many erefax to swallow, but integration into the Compact was otherwise relatively painless, even if the oldest erefax still grumble to this day about the Affini “moving too fast”.

Many cultural practices, including and especially that of crest-shaping, migrated from their original signifiers to being purely aesthetic practices. Crest-shaping and wool-shearing in particular are rolled into many of the kinky practices between erefax and Affini. As erefax are still a herd species, an affini will frequently find that an erefax floret has a large number of pinnates, and may take a multitude of them as florets at a time.

Erefax lanolin has euphoric, sedative and/or muscle relaxant effects on many other xenosophonts, and thus their wool is prized within the Compact as “dollwool”. This has led to popularity with dolls all over the compact.
