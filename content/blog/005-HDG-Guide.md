Hello, and welcome to the new HDG Community Guide! This site will serve as the new landing page and online reference for [[community|Human Domestication Guide]] as a community. Right now, some parts are still a work in progress, but already you'll be able to find basic information about the [[Affini]], [[Terrans]], a [[sophont-species|handful of other xenospecies]], and several other key topics. As the Guide grows, more and more will become available here, so check in regularly! 

Some of our older hands may be wondering why we've been putting so much effort into the new Guide when we already had a wiki, and it's a good question with a lot of answers. The biggest and most central is simply that the wiki was never meant to be a public reference, much less a landing page for the entire community and the first look newcomers got to Human Domestication Guide. This new site has been designed from the ground up to be public-facing, with an eye towards succinctly presenting relevant information and helping folks who know literally nothing about HDG find their footing quickly. 

I'm sure some are wondering, "Doesn't this mean that there's no way for us, the rest of the community, to add things to the site like we could with a wiki?" Well, first, the old wiki was never publicly editable (and, in fact, authentication was kind of a chore with it). However, the new Guide, while not directly web-editable, will in the future be opened to submissions from the community, either via a GitHub site or through the [HDG Community Discord](https://discord.gg/WgssQ6SR4q), which our team of [[glossary#L|Lorets]] will vet, approve, and merge. If anything, the new Guide will be a lot _more_ accessible to the community than the old wiki ever was! 

As mentioned above, the Guide is still a work in progress — you'll see a lot of TO-DO markers here and there, in fact — but it's finished enough for us to show it off a little and let folks get use out of the parts that are ready to go. In the near future we hope to add more detail on the main setting timeline, xenodrugs, in-depth looks at the [[affini-compact|Compact's]] Bureaucracy, and more! We hope you'll look forward to it, and to spending more time in this community we've all come to love! 

— Kanaĝen
