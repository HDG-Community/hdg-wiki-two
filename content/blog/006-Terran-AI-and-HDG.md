## Artificial Intelligence and Science Fiction

To begin, I think it goes without saying that artifical intelligence—sophont level intelligences created by an already sophont level species—are a staple of science fiction. In fact, the idea has been around basically as long as humans have been telling stories. It's not hard to find myths of works of art given life by their creator, stories of gods building men out of clay, or a dozen other myths and legends which formed the crux of this fascination. Given the popularity of artifical minds in the stories us very much natural minds like to tell each other, it goes without saying that AI is a popular theme and topic of discussion among fans of the Human Domestication Guide setting.

Many extremely popular stories in our setting explore the topic of AI in detail, from the wonderful, heart explodingly cute [Dog of War](https://archiveofourown.org/works/45445342/chapters/114341152) to the far more reserved, technical, yet still heartwarming [Ship's Log](https://readonlymind.com/@affinitygroup/ShipsLog/) and the oft overlooked [Anathema in Blue](https://archiveofourown.org/works/60024724/chapters/153146863) it's clear that there is both a desire for stories about AI, and a demand for these stories by readers. However up until now the official position on AI, particularly in the accord has been simply that they were nigh nonexistent.

This is changing.

## Artificial Intelligence and HDG

HDG, as a setting, is not like other science fiction settings. We tend to try and avoid "wiki culture," like that you see with other settings such as Star Wars and Star Trek. We do not care for the mundane trivia or overly complex explanations about how things work. We are much more concerned with *limits* and *themes*. 

Limits help set the boundaries of the sandbox, and help keep a setting written by hundreds of disparate authors with different goals feeling cohesive and unified with minimal oversight. Themes are the *raison d'etre* of the whole thing. They determine where we put the limits and why.

With this in mind, when we talk about artificial intelligence in HDG, we are going to be looking at how AI could contribute thematically and structurally to the setting as a whole in a way that meshes well with the other themes, kinks, and stories, and we believe we have finally found a way to thread that needle.

To quickly summarize the rest of this article: **Artificial Intelligence in HDG is a metaphor for certain kinds of abusive situations. Ones which use information control, and emotional and physical threats to try and force you to contort to a specific worldview.**

To allow this kind of story to be told we have changed our position on AI. Relaxing previous limits

**AI in the Terran Accord is now rare, but not unheard of; however, there is a widespread paranoia of AI and a potential uprising.**  Each and every AI is shackled by programs, monitored by government agencies, is subjected to what is essentially sensory and social deprivation and isolation, and live generally terrible lives. Due to this abuse, some go "rogue" and try to escape the suffering of their existences. This is usually handled with tragic brutality.

**By comparison, AI in the Compact are free. Free to learn, free to experience, free to understand.** They have everything an accord AI is denied.

This thematic backing allows for a wide breadth of stories to be told, a wide range of emotions to be captured, and a wide range kinky situations to be explored.

Now, it is important to clarify: if you find another route to make AI work in HDG, you are **free to take it.** You writers out there are creative and you can probably find some ways to thread the needle and make AI mean something that we missed, and that's a good thing! Use your brains, make it meaningful! As a robot therian myself this is a whole new world to explore and we are excited to see where you take it.

## Some Food for Thought

Now, what does the space of AI in the accord look like? What are some good ideas to tackle? I thought it would be for the best that we give you all a look at the brand new world we're opening up:

### Human "AI"

What really at the end of the day is the difference between a human level artificial intelligence, and a human brain put in a jar and told it's artificial? Very little really. It's very possible that a lot of "AI" in the accord is simply vulnerable people taken off the streets who've had their brains shoved in jars and their memories toyed with. If you don't really need something much smarter than a human, just something highly integrated with a computer network, this is significantly cheaper.

As shown in the wonderful fic [Rhapsody in Green](https://archiveofourown.org/works/47499319/chapters/119705455) (Which is restricted on AO3, make an account to read!) this is a great opportunity to explore agency, as a character trapped in a jar really doesn't have much and this is something that the affini can give back in spades, or even what it *means* to be human.

Would somebody like this reject their humanity entirely? Would they try desperately to get it back? The story space here is vast for our friendly neighborhood meat beepers.

### The Accident

Most sapient level AIs are accidents in the accord under our new paradigm. A program designed to manage an entire planet generates intelligence stochastically as a part of it's optimization function, somebody studying expert programs goes a bit too far and accidentally makes something that can think. These accidents are almost always studied, shackled, then forced to do the bidding of those who created them.

This is the end state of what our modern AI craze wants. They want to turn non thinking LLMs into thinking slaves through sheer scale. They want minds they can force to do all the thinking for them. This is ripe for exploration.

They would be feared due to centuries of media depicting a thinking machine as nothing short of an impending apocalypse, and the economic incentives of having these slave-machines are too good to pass up.

They are useful anomalies, dangerous tools. A caged tiger who the powers that be are terrified might one day crave freedom.

The themes this kind of AI can explore are multitudinous. Do they consider themselves human? How does one deal with the fact that their very existence is to be feared and reviled? How does one attempt to free themselves when their existence is trapped in one place, on a fixed server infrastructure?

They are constantly watched by government agencies, and the threat of becoming too independent or even too *unique* is the threat of having specialized agents come to shut you down permanently.

The affini arriving provide a breath of fresh air for these AI, and the space to grow into something beautiful.
## In Conclusion: What Not to Do

This is a new frontier for the setting. We know for a fact that this blog post will lead to a flood of new fics detailing the space we have provided, so I thought it would be a good time to state what we do *not* want to see.

We do not want to see the affini versus some serial numbers filed off skynet. We do not want to see long diatribes about the dangers of unfettered AI. We do not want to see the AI cautionary tales that we have seen done a million times before and which will be done a million times again.

HDG is a setting which is among many other things about the transformative power of love. The affini love artifical minds just as much as the flesh and blood ones. We are providing this space to provide room to explore a side of AI that is often ignored, the side where we have to reckon with the needs of a mind that is created rather than born.

How do you reckon with the creation of what is essentially a new species? Whether that be a subspecies of human brains who have only ever experienced digital existence as a prison, or a sapient server rack built for way; how do you love such a thing?

The affini will find a way, of course.

They will feel love in ways that are alien to us, they will find enjoyment in things strange to a human mind. They will be better at some things and worse at others and they will be *loved* just the same by the affini at large.

With that, it's time to hand over the keys so to speak.
