The Human domestication guide setting gains its unique feeling and texture from the technology and concepts that bring the world to life.
## About Technology
Within the context of Human Domestication Guide, two levels of technological development are important: the development of the Terran Accord, and the development of the Affini Compact. Other species may have variable levels of technological development, and may even be comparable to the Affini in some niche areas, but in general the Affini are the most technologically advanced culture in the setting. 

The [[terran-accord|Terran Accord]] has a grounded, hard sci-fi tone. Ships are powered using nuclear reactors, achieve thrust using conventional nuclear rocketry, have limited facilities for spin gravity, and are generally limited by physics as we currently understand them with some small exceptions (such as the jump drive, which even Terrans do not fully understand the operations of). Sidearms are more likely to be slugthrowers than energy weapons (although a few do exist in some stories). Cybernetic augmentations are possible but often have serious drawbacks, except for the extremely wealthy.

By contrast, the [[affini-compact|Affini Compact]], having been spacefaring for over 100,000 years and having acquired the technological corpus of every species they've domesticated over that period of time across multiple galaxies, may be fairly said to have achieved a level of technological development that is not only post-scarcity but downright utopian. They have functionally free energy, can produce any desired commodity on command, have mastered medical science to such a degree that disease is effectively eliminated, and so on. In short, their aesthetic is not hard sci-fi, but borderline magical; what limitations they do have are primarily there to keep the setting relatively grounded for the sake of narrative consistency. 

This technological gulf often serves as a contrast to heighten the sudden change and sense of shock that accompanies the arrival of the Affini, whether it be the shoddy medical care of the Accord contrasted with the loving care of a veterinarian or the wonder at Affini computing technology set against the backdrop of someone who's only ever known genAI-infested slopware. In narrative terms, it's one of the most visible markers and drivers of the oft-cited catch phrase "we never stood a chance."
## Setting Concepts 
Setting concepts serve as touchstones that are shared across otherwise disparate stories, the glue that holds our collective writing project together. Like technologies, they often serve as contrasts between the world of the Accord and the world of the Compact, a way to signpost that things are Different™ now that the Affini are here. This is true even for concepts or technologies that are shared between Accord and Compact; the Affini just do it better, in a way that the Terrans could never hope to.
## Technology and Concepts Glossary 
There are some pieces of technology, or concepts that are central to the Human Domestication Guide setting. While not every story will center them or even need to show them, the following are technological advancements or conceptual ideaforms that can reasonably be considered universal.
### Atomic Compilers 
**Affini Technology**

Atomic compilers are effectively the ultimate 3-D printer. Using advanced applications of the fifth fundamental force and a connection to a source of feedstock matter, a compiler is capable of rapidly creating virtually any physical object. This object can be organic or nonorganic, though while compilers can create things like meat, they cannot produce living tissue. Compilers can also *decompile* matter, deconstructing an object and returning its constituent materials to the feedstock pool. Safety interlocks prevent hazardous uses of this function. Every hab in the Affini Compact has a household compiler, roughly the size and shape of a large oven. Larger municipal compilers exist in the event that larger items are required. 

_Appear in many stories._
### Biomods
*Main Page: [[biomods|Biomods]]*

**Affini Technology**

Terran society had begun to experiment with cybernetics and aesthetic surgical alteration prior to the arrival of the Affini Compact, but in the wake of humanity's domestication, morphological freedom for terrans expanded in ways previously confined to the realm of dreams, wishes, and science fiction. Some levels of biomodification are accessible to any independent sophont; others only to florets.

*Appear in many stories.*
### Biorhythms 
**Concept** 

All living things have a biorhythm, made up of a combination of organic rhythms such as the heartbeat, respiration rate, etc, as well as of kinesthetic components such as facial expression, movement habits, and so on. Affini have especially strong biorhythms, which have evolved to be highly hypnotic; as such, even being in proximity to an Affini can have an effect on xenosophonts who are particularly susceptible. Biorhythms are also an important component in Affini communication, adding a complex undercurrent to whatever language is being used. 

_Appear in many stories, including [Ramifaction](https://archiveofourown.org/works/54481600/chapters/138022354)._
### Digitization
_Main Page: [[digitization|Digitization]]_

**Concept**

Digitization is the process of converting a non-digital (i.e., organic) sophont into a digital sophont, a thinking being that runs as a program on a computer. Some cultures develop this technology prior to the arrival of the Affini. In the Affini Compact, there are two primary means of digitization. The first is a copy & paste process of scanning a sophont's brain and starting up a new copy in digital space; the second is a process of haustoric implant overgrowth that converts a floret into a digital organism while preserving continuity of consciousness. 

_Appear in the [Digitization Writing Jam](https://archiveofourown.org/collections/HDG_Digitization_December_2025)_.
### Floret Cuts
**Concept** 

"Floret cut" is an unofficial jargon term used for media that has been edited to suit the needs and expectations of the individual watching it. Contrary to popular belief, this need not be for florets, though florets are often the target of this kind of media control. Undesirable elements (violence, capitalism, etc) may be seamlessly removed and replaced with narrative alternatives, characters may be altered to suit romantic or sexual preferences, and so on. This process can even be used to provide two individuals watching the same media on the same screen with different experiences through the use of lenticular angles and sonic interference. 

_Appear in many stories, including [From Pawn to Princess](https://archiveofourown.org/works/63477355/chapters/162653896)_.
### Jump Drives
**Technology**

By manipulating the spacetime metric (the "fabric" of spacetime), an object may enter an alternate dimension often referred to as hyperspace. Provided this is done with the appropriate force and geometries, this allows that object to pass through hyperspace and emerge into normal space once again, in the process bypassing the limitations of normal physics such as relativity. Virtually all faster-than-light travel in Human Domestication Guide uses a variant of this method, though few species have mastered it to the degree that the Affini have. 

_Appear in many stories, including [What Have We Learned?](https://archiveofourown.org/works/67250080)_.
### Ring Habitats 
**Technology**

Artificial gravity is a problem that many species struggle to solve, but a frequent stopgap employed by virtually every spacefaring sophont culture is the ring habitat. By spinning a wheel-or-cylinder-shaped drum, centrifugal force allows the interior surface of that drum to serve as relative "down," with variable gravity depending on size and rate of spin. Affini ships do not use artificial gravity, and instead are often constructed of multiple ring segments, allowing for variable gravity environments on a single ship. Large Terran ships often included much smaller gravity drums for crew habitat, though smaller ships often had to do without, and Terran space habitats (including their O'Neill Cylinders) frequently employed this technology as well. 

_Appear in many stories._
### Xenodrugs
_Main Page: [[xenodrugs|Xenodrugs]]_

**Affini Technology** 

Xenodrugs are specially tailored chemical compounds designed for use on xenosophont species, usually with application toward either recreational use or as a part of the individual domestication toolkit. Sorted into broad classes by general function, individual xenodrugs may have unusual side effects, meaning that one class-A trip may be very different from another. Affini often graft xenodrug-producing flora to themselves, giving them a chemical arsenal they carry with them everywhere, just in case it's needed. Xenodrugs are species-specific, though a given substance may have desirable effects on more than one xenosophont species. 

_Appear in many stories._
## Extended Glossary
The fun of a shared setting is that different authors can solve novel problems in different ways. The exact capabilities and unique cultural touchstones of the [[affini-compact|Affini Compact]] and the [[terran-accord|Terran Accord]] often vary story to story, so these can't be assumed to be universally present when reading, but they're all things you can pull from when writing. A technology or concept that appears in this list may eventually move up to the main glossary or get its own page if authors expand upon them!

> [!LORET]

- **Nothoculture:** A hybridization of Compact culture with xenosophont culture; the "best way" for a given species to live, according to Affini standards. Typically determined through sociological experimentation involving memory and behavioral alteration. _By Kanaĝen, seen in [A Twelvemonth and a Day](https://archiveofourown.org/works/66644596)_
- **Firebreak:** An application of ultrafine sensor grids and jump drive tech that disarms and defuses conventional weapons at range by throwing dangerous material into hyperspace. _By Annabool, seen in [Divaricated](https://readonlymind.com/@annabool/divaricated/)._
- **Printable Clothes:** Specialized compilers that can make clothing in place on a body that is too complex or difficult a material to conveniently put on normally, such as a latex bodysuit/ _By Kanagen, seen in [Lacuna](https://archiveofourown.org/works/72489471)._
