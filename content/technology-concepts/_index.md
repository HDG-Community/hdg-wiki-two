The Human domestication guide setting gains its unique feeling and texture from the technology and concepts that bring the world to life.

## About Technology
The [[terran-accord|Terran Accord]] has a grounded, hard scifi tone that undergoes a genre transition a more fantastical vision of the future within the [[affini-compact|Affini Compact]]. Having been around for approximately 100 thousand years and aquiring the accumulated advances of every species they have ever domesticated, the Affini Compact have a level of technology that has reached post-scarcity, and could be fairly described as utopian.

## Technology and Concepts Glossary 
There are some pieces of technology, or concepts that are central to the Human Domestication Guide setting. While not every story will center them or even need to show them, the following are technological advancements or conceptual ideaforms that can reasonably be considered universal.
### Biomods

**Affini Technology**

Terran society had begun to experiment with cybernetics and aesthetic surgical alteration prior to the arrival of the Affini Compact, but in the wake of humanity's domestication, morphological freedom for terrans expanded in ways previously confined to the realm of dreams, wishes, and science fiction. Some levels of biomodification are accessible to any independent sophont; others only to florets.

## Extended Glossary
The fun of a shared setting is that different authors can solve novel problems in different ways. The exact capabilities and unique cultural touchstones of the [[affini-compact|Affini Compact]] and the [[terran-accord|Terran Accord]] often vary story to story, so these can't be assumed to be universally present when reading, but they're all things you can pull from when writing. A technology or concept that appears in this list may eventually move up to the main glossary or get its own page if authors expand upon them!

> [!LORET]
> As this page is a **Work In Progress,** we have added a few examples we had ready below to showcase their formatting. We can't wait to add more!

- **Nothoculture:** A hybridization of Compact culture with xenosophont culture; the "best way" for a given species to live, according to Affini standards. Typically determined through sociological experimentation involving memory and behavioral alteration. _By Kanaĝen, seen in [A Twelvemonth and a Day](https://archiveofourown.org/works/66644596)_
- **Firebreak:** An application of ultrafine sensor grids and jump drive tech that disarms and defuses conventional weapons at range by throwing dangerous material into hyperspace. _By Annabool, seen in [Divaricated](https://readonlymind.com/@annabool/divaricated/)._
- **Printable Clothes:** Specialized compilers that can make clothing in place on a body that is too complex or difficult a material to conveniently put on normally, such as a latex bodysuit/ _By Kanagen, seen in [Lacuna](https://archiveofourown.org/works/72489471)._
