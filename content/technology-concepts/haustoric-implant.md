The Haustoric Implant is a phytotech implant inserted into the body of an affini's floret, which grows into them and supplements and/or supplants significant parts of the floret's nervous system. 

## Biotechnology and Development
Haustoric Implants are widely considered the peak of affini biotechnology, and provide an incredible array of information and powers to the affini who controls it. As such, development of a functional, reliable, safe haustoric implant is typically one of the top priorities in any [[florets-domestication|cotyledon program]]. Given the substantial positive health impacts of implantation, it is a non-negotiable part of taking a floret - any floret will, typically sooner rather than later, receive a haustoric implant. This procedure permanently links them to their affini, and serves as a metaphorical equivalent of a permanent wedding ring (and occasionally a new companion) for their floret. 

Each haustoric implant contains a piece of an affini's core, a "core sample", which is used to control and monitor the implant. This may be a single affini, or multiple affini, and if desired or needed, another affini's core sample may be added after implantation to allow the same degree of control. 

The expected failure rate for terran haustoric implants is roughly 50. As in, 50 total, across all terran implants, for the rest of time. This degree of safety is crucial, given how important florets are to affini and how important this procedure is to those florets.

Given the way the haustorium intertwines with the existing terran nervous system, and given the extremely positive effects of implantation, removing an implant is considered both functionally impossible and ethically reprehensible by affini. Additionally, given the benefits of implantation, delaying or refusing implantation is often seen by affini as irresponsible or even unethical or neglectful.

The Haustoric Implant is grown from a haustorium seed that has been combined with a sample from the owner affini's core, in a process that takes as little as a day and as long as a week depending on the given situation.

## Effects

The Haustoric Implant grows into and replaces large portions of a sophont's autonomic nervous system, and will overtake most autonomic functions (breathing, heartbeat, reflexes, etc.) with a much higher degree of effectiveness and reliability. As a result, aging is significantly slowed - an adult sophont, once implanted, can often expect their life expectancy to roughly *double* compared to an unimplanted member of their species. To take one example from terran xenopulmonology, the terran ailment of a heart attack is, with implant in place, effectively impossible. The implant can easily serve to strengthen the floret's musculature and pulmonary/cardiovascular systems, granting florets much stronger and more durable bodies than their unimplanted counterparts.

The implant also offers an extremely deep and granular pool of data about the functioning of the floret's body, as well as the floret's mental state. This makes it easy to detect shifts in mental or physical well-being, as well as making it possible to fully reconstruct a floret's conscious and subconscious thoughts. With the help of a haustoric implant, an affini can effectively read their floret's mind, to a far greater degree than the floret themselves can. 

It also offers a great deal of *control*. An affini can use the implant to, for example:
* pause their floret's heartbeat   
* force them to move in certain ways, say certain things, or otherwise puppet them  
* directly insert ideas into their conscious or subconscious mind  
* synthesize and deliver xenodrugs within their floret's body

...And much, much more. 

The core sample within the implant will also serve as a constant source of affini biorhythm, further attuning the floret to their owner(s), and ensuring that any dependency upon that biorhythm is sated while the floret is not directly present with their affini. 

Additionally, a haustoric implant serves as a key to unlocking many kinds of phytotech biomods that are inaccessible to independents. For more details, see [[biomods|Biomods]].

## Surgery and Recovery
The Haustoric Implant is inserted into a floret's spine, typically through a procedure fittingly known as the Implantation Surgery. This procedure is routine and safe, commonly completed while the floret is unconcious thanks to the use of a [[xenodrugs|Class-Z Xenodrug]]. On rare occasion, some florets elect to receive their implant while conscious, or receive the implant in an atypical delivery method.

It will typically take a few weeks to recover from Implantation Surgery while the implant grows in. During this time, the floret in question will typically be held on a regimen of class-J and class-M xenodrugs and doted on constantly by their affini, to ensure that the process is disturbed as little as possible. During this period, it is common for the terran to experience vivid “implant dreams”, representative of the implant fusing with their nervous system.
