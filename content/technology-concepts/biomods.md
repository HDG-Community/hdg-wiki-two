Terran society had begun to experiment with cybernetics and aesthetic surgical alteration prior to the arrival of the Affini Compact, but in the wake of humanity's domestication, morphological freedom for terrans expanded in ways previously confined to the realm of dreams, wishes, and science fiction. The Cotyledon Program taught the Affini virtually everything they needed to know about how the human body works, and what they didn't learn in those three brief years, they learned in the years immediately following the folding of Terran space into the Compact. 

There are, roughly speaking, five tiers of potential body modification available to terrans under the Compact, increasing in phenotypic divergence from the human norm with each step. The first two tiers are accessible to any independent sophont; the latter three are available only to florets, as they rely on the presence of a haustoric implant to function. 
## Xenodrugs Alone
{{< image biomod_one_sheepwave "Text: Xenodrugs Alone. Images of three humanoids with minor modifications." inline >}}
    Art by [Sheepwave](https://archiveofourown.org/users/sheepwave/works).
{{< /image >}}

Class-G and Class-V xenodrugs represent the first tier of body alteration available to sophonts in the Affini Compact. This is a purely chemical intervention, prescribed by a veterinarian, and can alter minor details about a sophont's body. 

What is minor, in this context? For a human, this reflects things like secondary sexual characteristics, hair growth patterns, eye color, shifts in height of no more than 10-15% of the sophont's natural height, muscle development, minor phenotype alterations, and the like. Anything a human body can naturally do, Class-G xenodrugs can do; Class-V xenodrugs can even push the envelope somewhat, but purely chemical changes remain limited. 

As a rule of thumb, anything that a particularly skill ren-fair makeup artist could do, xenodrugs alone can do on a permanent basis. This is, however, the absolute limit of what a xenodrug regimen can do; for anything more, including changes to internal organs and reproductive capabilities, surgery is required. 
## Unimplanted Surgery
{{< image biomod_two_sheepwave "Text: Unimplanted Surgery. Images of a horned devil, human with balljoint, catgirl" inline >}}
    Art by [Sheepwave](https://archiveofourown.org/users/sheepwave/works).
{{< /image >}}

Affini surgical techniques are far in advance of Terran ones, even the Terran medical science of the 2550s. Many surgeries available at this tier are outpatient surgeries, with only the most demanding and extensive modifications requiring veterinary hospital stays or a medical wardship. 

Surgeries can add many traits to the human body that xenodrugs cannot, such as horns, tails, various animal ears, and so on. Generally speaking, anything that the human brain retains structures for (such as a tail) can be easily grafted. Structures that the human brain _doesn't_ retain the ability to natively operate, such as cat ears, would require physical therapy to be mobile. This means, among other things, that prosthetics in the Compact are far superior to any developed by the Accord, able to replace function and sensation on a 1:1 basis.

Surgery can also implant specialized organs and symbiotes. These might be automated xenodrug reservoirs, subcutaneous imaging symbiotes for programmable or animated tattoos, and so forth. These are typically phytotech devices, and are not innately controlled by the terran hosting them through a direct neurological link; instead, a control interface such as a tablet would be necessary, though some might operate on biofeedback principles (such as releasing analgesics during a chronic pain flareup). 

While the limitations of this tier _can_ be exceeded, this is not typical practice: adding third-tier or higher implants and modifications without a haustoric implant to govern them requires grueling physical therapy to achieve any control whatsoever, and will likely never equate to the intuitive experience of using the modification through a haustoric implant.

Older stories from the era before HDG moved to Ao3 may say that Class-V xenodrugs can accomplish things in this category, but the shared setting has mostly moved on from this, with Class-V generally acting as immunosuppressants and neurological integrators if mentioned at all.
## Surgery With Haustorium
{{< image biomod_three_sheepwave "Text: Surgery with Haustorium. Images of full doll, plantvine body, glowing purple exoskeleton" inline >}}
    Art by [Sheepwave](https://archiveofourown.org/users/sheepwave/works).
{{< /image >}}

The haustoric implant is rightly considered the crowning achievement of Affini bioscience, a device that inserts itself into and hybridizes with neurological tissue, eventually replacing the peripheral nervous system entirely. Because the haustoric implant can act as a neurological router, it can serve as a shortcut to adapting new nervous system infrastructure without physical therapy. This allows a floret to accept implants that radically alter function of the body.

Importantly, the limits on implants conforming to human evolutionary history and brain structure are removed at this tier. This allows changes ranging from hot-swappable or self-transforming limbs to functional wings to sensory alteration and augmentation and beyond. Any physiology that the floret can physically host can be connected to their brain through the haustoric implant; some florets have the majority of their body replaced with phytotech constructs. 

The only real limitation on what alterations a floret can host is that, ultimately, there's a human brain in the mix. Everything else is fair game. If that isn't enough, there's always the next tier, an even more significant step away from the human baseline than hosting a haustoric implant. 
## Phytotech Body
{{< image biomod_four_sheepwave "Text: Phytotech Body. Images of tiny humanoid, slimegirl and robot chasis" inline >}}
    Art by [Sheepwave](https://archiveofourown.org/users/sheepwave/works).
{{< /image >}}

_Main Article: [[digitization|Digitization]]_

A digitized sophont is either a copy of a biological sophont produced by a scanning process (a form of digitization available to independents), or a floret whose brain has been converted into a phytotech computer through the overgrowth process, preserving continuity of consciousness. Both this tier and the subsequent, ultimate tier of morphological freedom are available to both kinds of digital sophont. 

Where a biological floret is limited by the need to carry around and keep a physical, biological brain alive, a digitized floret can take on virtually any shape while interacting with the physical world. They do so by streaming their consciousness through a phytotech construct, essentially a full-body-replacement version of the previous tier, though without any constraints beyond the laws of physics themselves. At this tier, it's possible to have a body only a few inches tall, a body made entirely of amorphous programmable matter, or a chassis that is openly and obviously machine in nature. This tier is also the realm of sophont-as-infrastructure, wherein a mind is streamed through a starship, a factory, or another, similar construct totally divorced from even the concept of a biological sophont. Temporary phytotech bodies can also be connected to implanted but undigitized florets with the use of a sensorium streaming mod, at the cost of fidelity and significant oversight by thier owner.

And if physics is _still_ too onerous an obstacle... 
## Digital Avatar
{{< image biomod_five_sheepwave "Text: Digital Avatar. Images of gas polygons, polygonal teapot humanoid, abstract data creature" inline >}}
    Art by [Sheepwave](https://archiveofourown.org/users/sheepwave/works).
{{< /image >}}

Many digital sophonts retain a form similar to the one they enjoyed in their biological life, but just as many throw convention to the wind and embrace the infinite possibility of digital existence. Digital sophonts might inhabit avatars that are nothing more than a whirling collection of crystals, a living sine wave, a reflection in a convenient surface, or anything else they can dream of and code for themselves or have coded for them. Digital sophonts might eschew avatars entirely and exist as pure code, or they might exist as entire simulated worlds inhabited by other digital sophonts. 

Biological sophonts can, to a certain degree, replicate the experience of the digital tiers of morphological freedom through extensive xenodrug-amplified hypnosis and full-dive VR rigs, but this is a temporary condition that must be carefully managed by an Affini handler, and not a native part of their existence.
