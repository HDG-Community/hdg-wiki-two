So you saw someone, somewhere, talking about Human Domestication Guide and, wondering what the hell it even is, wandered through the GenAI-infested dreck of the search engine of your choice until you found your way here. Or maybe you found your own way, surfing through stories on Archive of Our Own or ScribbleHub or elsewhere, with the same results. Either way, you're here, you're confused, you're maybe a little upset, and you have to know: 

_"How the fuck are people getting off to this?!"_

Yeah, look, we know we kinda have a reputation as a bunch of evangelizing weirdos who Will Not Shut The Fuck Up about our favorite dub/non-con imperialism alien invasion setting. HDG has this weird effect on certain people where, once they discover it, they go into full hyperfocus mode and rapidly spiral down the rabbit-hole, reading every single story they can get their hands on. There is a reason that our Discord server is overwhelmingly neurospicy trans folks, and like a lot of neurospicy folks, when we discover something we love we kinda tend to go all in on it. 

As a result, there's a peripheral fandom around the actual HDG writing community (yes, these are two different things) that floods social media feeds with discussion of the setting, goes into twitch chats and other parasocial spaces and eagerly asks total strangers "Hey, have you read Human Domestication Guide?" and so on. They do not, however, tend to explain what the hell HDG actually is, leading to more people encountering it and being absolutely baffled by it, not having the context to understand what the hell is going on, and consequently reading it as kinda fucked up. 

And, yeah, it _is_ fucked up. That's kinda the point. It's niche erotica written by and _for_ an audience of mostly disabled queer autistic trans folks. We are taking the shit we deal with in our daily lives, the institutional trauma and experience of being disabled, trans, etc in modern Western society, and eroticizing it. The fucked-up-edness is the point. We're kinking on it. It is not a manifesto. We are not advocating that humans treat each other this way. The point of the Affini is very much that they _aren't_ human, that they're more a force of nature, that they're frankly just a little bit impossible in behavioral terms, because _that's what's necessary to make the systems we're forced to interact with benevolent_. Which is the whole point of the exercise. 

If you encounter HDG and you don't understand its appeal, it's probably because you don't fit into one or more of those groups. We're the focal point of a very specific Venn diagram here, and we understand that. It's one reason we do our level best to limit the spread of HDG, and yes, really. The amount of HDG out there you see in transfem spaces is _after_ we've specifically asked folks not to go around evangelizing about it. The lack of any large-scale deep-dive analysis media by content creators is, again, the result of us specifically saying "We really do not want that kind of attention" when they ask about us. 

Like, we get it. It's _weird_. But we like the weirdness, and we're having fun on our own over here, and we're not trying to force others to enjoy our fun with us. If you want to, hey, cool, more friends, more creativity. We love that! But if it's not your jam, it's not your jam, and that's okay too. 

In short, please do not confuse fangirls posting "please step on me plommy" on social media with the setting's actual authors unironically IRL supporting any of the ethically dubious at best content of their work. We're not running a cult or anything, we're just writing sci-fi bodice rippers for each other.
