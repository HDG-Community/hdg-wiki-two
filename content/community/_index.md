If you're here, reading this, then you've probably at least heard of HDG. You may not, however, have really had it all explained to you! This page will give you a crash course in what the HDG Community is, its various permutations, and what we're even doing here. 

Note: If you're totally lost or have been deluged with a list of demands to read HDG and you're just feeling very confused and want to skip the history lesson, you might want to check out [[a-normies-guide-to-hdg|A Normie's Guide to HDG]]. 
## What IS HDG? 
Human Domestication Guide is a shared creative setting created by GlitchyRobo, the subject of which is the conquest and domestication of humanity in the 2550s by a species of intergalactic imperialist post-scarcity aliens called [[Affini]]. The Affini Compact is a cultural-level machine dedicated to the idea that no sophont, anywhere, should suffer, and that the Affini are uniquely positioned to carry out that goal. The Compact has, over the last 100,000 Earth years, expanded from its roots in the Triangulum Galaxy across all of Andromeda and finally to the Milky Way. The Affini are benevolent, at least, by their definition of benevolence. They are also fundamentally unstoppable. They _will_ make you happy; the only question is, how much will you have to change to get there? 

That's the elevator pitch for the setting. While the [[writing-in-hdg|Writing in HDG]] page has a little more detail on what that all means, this is more of an overview to help you understand what we're doing here. 
## What ARE You All Doing Here?
Mostly, making and enjoying very niche non/dub-con erotica. We also complain about capitalism and hetero/cis/neuronormativity a lot. If you like doing those things, you'll fit right in! If not, well, maybe you should have clicked on [[a-normies-guide-to-hdg|A Normie's Guide to HDG]] up at the top of the page.
## Is This a Cult or Manifesto or Something? 
No, we just like smut. Think of it like bodice-ripper romance novels, but it's got an alien in it. Also philosophy. For some reason we have a lot of philosophy in our smut. 

Because some folks still don't get it unless we specifically say it: We do _not_ encourage folks to try to replicate Affini/floret relationships IRL. Please remain safe, sane, and consensual in your play. 
## How Did This Happen? 
[Human Domestication Guide](https://archiveofourown.org/works/45190954/chapters/113686849) was an idea long before it was a story. Initially told in bits and bobs of lore on a Mastodon timeline, eventually the original story went up on a relatively niche mind-control story archive (no, not that one). It quickly garnered attention from other writers who were fascinated by the setting and the concept, some of whom wanted to write their own take on the setting. This small core of writers coalesced at first in a Discord group DM; however, it very quickly outgrew that space. 
### The Canon List Era 
[The HDG Community Discord](https://discord.gg/WgssQ6SR4q) was established not long after. This became, and remains to this day, the primary hub for creatives interested in participating in the community, as well as just a fun place for folks interested in HDG to hang out and talk about our favorite foliage. For a while, the [[glossary#L|Lorets]] maintained the practice of checking new submissions to the setting for canon compliance, but as the number of participants in the collective writing project grew, this task became more than they could handle. Indeed, HDG became so popular so fast that in early 2023 the site acting as host for pretty much all HDG fics to that point asked us to leave, because we were drowning out literally everything else being submitted. 

So, in need of a place to put new stories, we turned to the one place on the internet where absolutely anyone can post absolutely anything fiction-related. 
### The Archive of Our Own Era 
Once HDG began being regularly posted on Archive of Our Own, readership grew rapidly — and, unsurprisingly, many of the new readers wished to contribute their own takes on HDG as well. This effect quickly snowballed, and the HDG Community Discord went from a few hundred members to _thousands_ seemingly overnight. 

Around this time, the Lorets began to reevaluate community management. The old Verified Canon List was not really practical any longer, with multiple new stories being added every single day. The decision was made to close the list, and to provide instead a series of [[writing-in-hdg|Axioms and Rules]] to help show writers where the center of gravity of HDG's themes and mood lie. By breaking out Lore into a separate category altogether, and eventually breaking it down into discrete levels of Lore with varying flexibility, the setting itself became even more of a living document than it already was. Later, a list of [[foundational-works|Foundational Works]] was created to provide a (relatively) quick introduction to the core of HDG as written. 

More readers continued to discover HDG, and more of them wanted to write for it, and, well, here we are — a community of thousands united by our love of the kinky space imperialism plants. 
### The Community Today 
The HDG Community is, of course, more than simply the stories and the [Discord](https://discord.gg/WgssQ6SR4q). Pockets of HDG lovers can be found all over social media, from Tumblr to Bluesky and beyond. These folks are just as much a part of the broader community as regulars in the Discord, forming a peripheral community around the highly active and creatively focused core. 

While we Lorets can only really speak for the Discord and its broader staff, we think that's great. We love seeing new folks find HDG and fall down the rabbit-hole, and we love helping foster a sense of shared creativity around the setting and the stories. We welcome participation in the community from all corners, but there's some things you should probably know about before you dive in headfirst.
