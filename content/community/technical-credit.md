## Fonts:
### Atkinson Hyperlegible (V11 & Next)
Fonts designed by the Braille Institute of America to be as legible and readable as possible for folks with low vision. (And we think they just kinda look really nice!)  
[Website](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/) | [SIL OPEN FONT LICENSE Version 1.1](https://braileinstitute.app.box.com/s/rin3vzegmcy7sil28yfqslz2r5etv5nl)
### Fantasque Sans Mono
A monospace font designed primarially for programmers. Functional, but with a nice bit of "wibbly-wobbly handwriting-like fuzziness".  
[Website](https://github.com/belluzj/fantasque-sans) | [SIL OPEN FONT LICENSE Version 1.1](https://github.com/belluzj/fantasque-sans?tab=OFL-1.1-1-ov-file#readme)
